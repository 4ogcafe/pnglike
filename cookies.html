1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites that you visit. At PngLike (https://pnglike.com), we believe in privacy-by-design. We use standard browser LocalStorage and essential HTTP cookies solely to keep you signed in and preserve your UI preferences (such as Dark Mode).

Zero Invasive Ad Trackers: We do not use third-party marketing cookies, cross-site trackers, or data broker pixels. Your browsing behavior across other websites is never tracked by PngLike.

2. Storage Keys Used by PngLike

Key Name Type Purpose Lifespan
pnglike_user_token LocalStorage Authenticates your signed-in registered session and unlocks 100 daily generations. 30 Days
pnglike_admin_token LocalStorage Stores encrypted administrator authorization token for CMS management. 7 Days
pnglike_theme LocalStorage Remembers your preferred color theme (Dark or Light Mode). Persistent

3. How to Manage or Clear Cookies

You can clear your browser storage or delete individual cookies at any time via your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Clear Browsing Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.

Note: Clearing local storage will sign you out of your account and reset your theme preference to dark mode.
