Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 06, 2026 • Last Updated: November 06, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to PngLike ("we", "our", or "us"), powered by Div Sri. We provide a professional 9-in-1 AI image editing, portrait retouching, super-resolution upscaling, and neural background removal platform accessible at https://pnglike.com/.

We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Our systems are engineered so that your creative files remain your own. We do not sell your photos, monetize your private image content, train public foundation models on your private uploads without consent, or share your identity with advertising networks.

2. In-Memory Image Processing & Ephemeral Storage

When you upload an image for background removal, upscaling, or any of the 9 studio tools:

  • Processing in Memory: Your uploaded images are decoded and processed directly in volatile server memory using local ONNX Runtime inference sessions and NumPy/Pillow computer vision pipelines.
  • Guest Processing: For unregistered guest users, image buffers are discarded immediately after the processed response is streamed back to your browser. No persistent cloud copies remain on our disk.
  • Registered Accounts: When logged into a registered account, processed images and telemetry (dimensions, processing time, format) are stored in your private cloud gallery on our MySQL database so you can re-download or manage your work. You can delete these records at any time from your User Dashboard.

3. What Information We Collect

Account Information

Username, email address, salted password hash (never plaintext), optional bio, and display preferences.

Quota & Telemetry Data

Client IP addresses are used solely for enforcing fair daily rate limits (3 jobs/day for guests) and defending against automated bot attacks.

4. No Facial Recognition or Biometric Profiling

Our portrait tools (such as Skin Enhancer and Relight) utilize frequency-domain texture detection and directional light maps. We do not extract biometric facial geometries, build facial recognition databases, or identify individual persons in photos.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance (Your Rights)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain full authority over your data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can download a complete machine-readable copy of your profile and history directly via the GET /api/user/export endpoint in your Dashboard.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit your name, bio, and preferences in your Dashboard at any time.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request full deletion of your user account and job history.
  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser Local Storage exclusively for functional operations: remembering your session tokens (pnglike_user_token), theme preferences (light/dark), and temporary quota display. For complete details, review our Cookie Policy.
