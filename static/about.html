About PngLike AI Studio

Next-generation 9-in-1 neural image processing workstation, created and powered by Div Sri. Built for privacy, extreme precision, and effortless creativity.

Our Vision & Mission

Graphic designers, e-commerce sellers, content creators, and photographers have spent countless hours manually tracing pixel masks and wrestling with expensive, subscription-locked image editors that upload private files to opaque clouds.

PngLike was conceived by Div Sri with a singular mission: build an open, accessible, high-performance image suite combining deep learning computer vision (U2-Net, IS-Net, Silueta) with immediate client responsiveness, zero mandatory subscriptions, and an uncompromising commitment to user privacy.

The 9-in-1 AI Tool Suite

1. AI Cutout

U2-Net neural background removal with sub-pixel alpha matting.

2. 4x Upscaler

Lanczos super-resolution with bilateral edge enhancement.

3. Image Extender

Contextual outpainting expanding canvas to 16:9, 9:16, and 21:9.

4. Variations

Artistic style transfers (Cyberpunk, Anime, Watercolor, Sketch).

5. Cinematic Shot

Optical depth-of-field bokeh, anamorphic lens flares, and LUT grading.

6. Skin Enhancer

Blemish smoothing with high-frequency skin texture preservation.

7. 3D Studio Relight

Directional key, rim, and dual-neon lighting on isolated subjects.

8. Change Camera

Perspective homography tilt, pan, and 16mm fisheye / telephoto lenses.

9. Image Editor

Complete studio color grading, exposure, saturation, warmth, and crop.
